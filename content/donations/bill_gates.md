# Internal Notes

Gates Foundation contributions are reconstructed year by year from the foundation's own statements: annual-report statements of activities for 1998–2006, the Trust's audited financial statements (contributions note) for 2007–2010, 2016, and 2018, and the Trust's Form 990-PF for the remaining years. For 990-PF years without an audited breakdown, Bill and Melinda's amount is the Part I line 1 total minus Warren Buffett's audited gift that year. That method reproduces the audited breakdown plus investment management fees in every year where both exist (2015, 2019–2023). Melinda French Gates's own post-divorce gifts to the Trust are in `melinda_gates.md`.

Downstream foundation grants (UNCF, Gavi, PATH, university, and library grants) are deliberately absent: the money was counted when it entered the foundation, so recording the grants too would double-count it. The 2017 Dementia Discovery Fund item was a venture investment, not a donation.

Reconciliation against the fact sheet (Bill Gates and Melinda French Gates total giving to the foundation, inception through 2025: $63.9B; through 2024: $60.2B): this file plus `melinda_gates.md` record about $61.8B including the Gates Library Foundation row. The ~$2.1B remainder is unresolved: contributions before April 1997 and during the 1998 stub periods not covered by the surviving statements, the $624.5M gap between the 1999 narrative and audited figures, investment management fees for 2016–2018, and any 2017 contributions beyond the Microsoft share gift. The Trust's 2016–2018 returns are not in the IRS e-file data. Fill these from primary documents rather than plugging the difference.

The September 2026 data corrections (Buffett annual series, removal of downstream grants, filing-exact amounts, the four-way Pivotal split) originate from JP Addison's fork, `jpaddison3/charity-adjusted-billionaires`.
