# Internal Notes

Identity and living status: the SEC/IAPD record gives his full name as Benjamin Henry Hoskin and lists current employment at Value Aligned Research Advisors. VARA's March 2026 Form ADV data also lists the firm as active. Sources: https://reports.adviserinfo.sec.gov/reports/individual/individual_7508714.pdf and https://reports.adviserinfo.sec.gov/reports/ADV/319135/PDF/319135.pdf

Birth date: no reliable birth date was found. Oxford records identify Hoskin as a first-year undergraduate at Lady Margaret Hall in February 2010, and the college's alumni book places him in its 2009 cohort. Those facts suggest a birth year around 1990 or 1991. `1991-01-01` is an approximate placeholder and should not be interpreted as his actual birthday. Sources: https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/system/files/legacy/3403/JCCU%20Minutes%2005.02.10.pdf and https://www.lmh.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/documents/2023-10/LMH%20The%20Brown%20Book%202023%20-%20Accessible.pdf

Net worth: no Forbes, Bloomberg, financial disclosure, or other reliable published estimate of Hoskin's personal wealth was found. The $20 million figure is a low-confidence editorial estimate based on his finance career since 2013 and his approximately half ownership of VARA, which was founded in 2022. VARA's March 2026 Form ADV reported $9.14 billion of regulatory assets under management, including $5.19 billion in its private fund, and reported that the adviser and related persons beneficially owned approximately 5% of that fund. June 2026 trade reporting subsequently put the private fund at approximately $15 billion following unusually strong reported returns. Assets under management belong to clients, and the public record does not disclose how the related-person stake is divided, VARA's fee terms and expenses, carried-interest allocations, taxes, or Hoskin's other assets and liabilities, so the estimate may be materially wrong. Sources: https://reports.adviserinfo.sec.gov/reports/ADV/319135/PDF/319135.pdf, https://www.9at.com/Adviser/801-123703, and https://www.withintelligence.com/insights/focus-on-ai-companies-fuels-fastest-growing-hedge-fund-managers-in-industrys-history/

Donation reconciliation: MIRI's April 30, 2026 contributor page reports $94,369 for Hoskin, while the donation tracker reconstructs five gifts totaling $94,209 from archived MIRI pages. MIRI says its displayed totals include matching funds leveraged, so the unexplained $160 difference is not recorded as a separate transfer from Hoskin. The MIRI rows use the dates of the cited archived-page captures as proxies because exact transfer dates are not public. Sources: https://intelligence.org/topcontributors/ and https://donations.vipulnaik.com/donor.php?donor=Ben+Hoskin

Research scope and exclusions: The separately reported $20,000 CHAI gift is included. Hoskin served as an S-process recommender for Survival and Flourishing Fund grants, but those grants came from the funders, not from him, and are not credited to Hoskin. No personal foundation, donor-advised fund, attributable political giving, or other amount-specific gifts were found in the sources reviewed. Source: https://survivalandflourishing.fund/2021/h1/recommendations
