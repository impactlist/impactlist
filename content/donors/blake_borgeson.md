# Internal Notes

Identity and living status: Borgeson's current personal site describes his work at Recursion, BuildASign, MIRI, and Good Forever. Recursion's 2026 proxy lists him as a current director and co-founder. Sources: https://www.blakeb.org/ and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601830/000160183026000065/rxrx-20260430.htm

Birth date: Recursion's proxy reported that Borgeson was 44 on April 21, 2026, which bounds his birth date to April 22, 1981–April 21, 1982. `1981-10-21` is the neutral midpoint of that interval and should not be interpreted as his actual birthday. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601830/000160183026000065/rxrx-20260430.htm

Net worth: no reliable published estimate of Borgeson's total wealth was found. The $25 million figure is a low-confidence editorial estimate. A July 2026 SEC filing showed 6,218,287 Recursion shares after a sale at $3.9625 per share, valuing that disclosed stake at about $24.6 million at the transaction price. Borgeson has also sold Recursion shares and had proceeds from earlier businesses and investments, but his taxes, liabilities, private holdings, and spending are unknown. Good Forever Foundation's assets are irrevocably charitable and are excluded. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1856094/000185609426000011/0001856094-26-000011-index.htm

Donation reconciliation: the 17 recorded rows total $13,075,449. Of this, $12,488,999 represents documented contributions to Good Forever or conservative floors on earlier contributions to donor-advised funds that later granted to Good Forever. The $4,238,107 of Fidelity Charitable and Silicon Valley Community Foundation floors remain recorded at a donor-specific DAF recipient because that is when the money left Borgeson's control; the recipient uses Good Forever's category split because all observed downstream funding went there. The transfers to Good Forever are not counted again. The Austin Community Foundation DAF row similarly uses CFAR's AI-risk classification without recording its downstream grant as another donation. Good Forever's separately itemized $189,490 stock gift remains a direct Borgeson contribution. Sources: https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/873744128, https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/110303001/202521349349310887/full, https://www.svcf.org/assets/img/media/SVCF-2023-990-PDC.pdf, and https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/741934031/202113199349318561/full

Research scope and exclusions: Good Forever's grants out are not counted again. This includes the $400,000 Borgeson column in SFF's 2024 flexHEGs recommendations, which reconciles to Good Forever's grant list, as well as grants to MIRI, CFAR, FAR AI, Palisade Research, Topos Institute, and other organizations. A reported $90,000 CFAR figure for 2016 is an arithmetic estimate from Borgeson's intended percentage allocation, not evidence of a completed transfer, and is excluded; the separately documented $75,000 Austin Community Foundation DAF floor is recorded instead. Political contributions, mission-related investments, for-profit investments, Recursion stock sales, and support with no public amount are excluded.
