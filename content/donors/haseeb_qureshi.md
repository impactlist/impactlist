# Internal Notes

Living status: Dragonfly currently lists Qureshi as a managing partner, and he remained publicly active in that role in 2026.

Birth date: Qureshi wrote on January 6, 2020 that he had recently turned 30, and his biography says he turned 21 in 2011. Those statements support a 1990 birth year, but no reliable month or day was found. `1990-01-01` is an approximate placeholder and should not be interpreted as his actual birthday: https://haseebq.com/another-decade/ and https://haseebq.com/about/

Net worth: no Forbes, Bloomberg, financial disclosure, or other reliable published estimate of Qureshi's personal wealth was found. The $50 million figure is a low-confidence editorial estimate based on his service as a Dragonfly managing partner since 2019, the firm's approximately $4 billion under management as of February 2026, and its $650 million third and fourth venture funds. Fund assets belong to limited partners, not Qureshi, and management fees, carried interest, ownership shares, taxes, and liquidity are not public, so this figure may be materially wrong. Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-build-vc-firm-haseeb-qureshi-kktwf and https://medium.com/dragonfly-research/announcing-dragonfly-fund-iii-7f50a962f40c

Research scope and exclusions: Qureshi's current biography says he has donated 33% of his income every year since 2015, but recipient and amount information was not found after the separately documented December 2020 SENS gift. Those later gifts are excluded rather than invented. His Founders Pledge and 2021 Crypto Giving Pledge are commitments, not completed transfers, and are also excluded. In 2013 he said he disposed of about $500,000 in total assets, but only $75,000 went to charity; the remainder transferred to his family and is not counted. No personal foundation, independently attributable political giving, or additional completed gifts were found in the sources reviewed.
