# Internal Notes

Identity and living status: Habryka's current LessWrong profile says he runs Lightcone Infrastructure, LessWrong, the AI Alignment Forum, Lighthaven, and Lightspeed Grants. A 2021 announcement says he founded the LessWrong 2.0 team in 2017 and reorganized it as Lightcone Infrastructure, with himself as CEO. Companies House lists Oliver Lorenz Habryka as an active director, a German national, and a United States resident. Sources: https://www.lesswrong.com/users/habryka4/, https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/eR7Su77N2nK3e5YRZ/the-lesswrong-team-is-now-lightcone-infrastructure-come-work-3, and https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/officers/yGQYR2060O2R0Xy047-Z3wAaS8Y/appointments

Birth date: Companies House reports only April 1995, not a day. `1995-04-15` is a neutral mid-month placeholder and should not be interpreted as his actual birthday. Source: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/officers/yGQYR2060O2R0Xy047-Z3wAaS8Y/appointments

Net worth: no reliable published estimate of Habryka's personal wealth was found. The $1 million figure is a low-confidence editorial estimate based on his nonprofit career, his documented $290,750 of Lightcone giving, and his statement that the main gift came from appreciated cryptocurrency. Habryka has also said that he loaned all of his personal assets to Lightcone and fully forgone his salary when the organization ran out of cash. Lightcone and Lighthaven assets belong to the nonprofit and are not treated as his wealth; the public record does not disclose the size or terms of his loan, his remaining crypto holdings, taxes, liabilities, or other assets, so the estimate may be materially wrong. Sources: https://www.lesswrong.com/leaderboard, https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5n2ZQcbc7r4R8mvqc/the-lightcone-is-nothing-without-its-people?commentId=PH6iCjWdaGJ3ow89v, and https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Bxv69g6MKhdEzoxTh/the-deep-lore-of-lighthaven-with-oliver-habryka-tbc-episode

Future research: if Habryka later forgives any outstanding balance of the personal-assets loan instead of being repaid, treat the forgiven principal as a potential additional donation once the waiver and amount are documented.

Donation reconciliation: LessWrong's current leaderboard attributes $290,750 to Habryka. He wrote in December 2024 that he had donated about $300,000 to Lightcone at the end of 2023 to keep it afloat, and a follow-up comment says the gift used appreciated cryptocurrency. Manifund separately records an exact $750 self-donation to Lightcone on September 4, 2024. The files therefore record $290,000 at the end of 2023 plus the $750 Manifund gift, exactly reconciling to the leaderboard total without double-counting. Manifund also records a separate $50 personal gift to Fatebook and Quantified Intuitions on September 16, 2024, bringing known lifetime giving to $290,800. Habryka said again in July 2026 that his Lightcone giving over the preceding few years substantially exceeded his aggregate salary, but gave no new amount; that statement is treated as corroboration of the documented total rather than as an additional gift. Sources: https://www.lesswrong.com/leaderboard, https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5n2ZQcbc7r4R8mvqc/the-lightcone-is-nothing-without-its-people?commentId=6wJhGhHwyC9v69wNW, https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5n2ZQcbc7r4R8mvqc/the-lightcone-is-nothing-without-its-people?commentId=PH6iCjWdaGJ3ow89v, https://manifund.org/projects/lightcone-infrastructure, https://manifund.org/projects/fatebook-and-quantified-intuitions, and https://x.com/ohabryka/status/2080795510731059329

Research scope and exclusions: the current and past MIRI contributor lists do not name Habryka, and no amount-specific personal gifts beyond Lightcone and Fatebook and Quantified Intuitions were found.
