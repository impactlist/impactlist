# Internal Notes

This recipient represents an unobservable contribution into a donor-advised fund at Austin Community Foundation. The amount is a conservative floor based on the foundation's FY2020 Schedule I, which labels a $75,000 grant to the Center for Applied Rationality as a "Blake Borgeson donation." The row remains at the DAF under the record-money-once policy, while its `ai-risk: 1.0` allocation follows the documented destination and CFAR's recipient mapping. The original DAF contribution date and any other Borgeson-advised grants are not public.
