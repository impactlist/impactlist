# Internal Notes

The Manifund gift is earmarked for Sage Future's Fatebook and Quantified Intuitions project rather than for Sage's broader portfolio. Fatebook helps people and organizations record and resolve probabilistic predictions; Quantified Intuitions provides forecasting, calibration, and estimation training with rapid feedback. The gift therefore maps entirely to decision-making. Sources: https://manifund.org/projects/fatebook-and-quantified-intuitions, https://fatebook.io/about, and https://sage-future.org/
