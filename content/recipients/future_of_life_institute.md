# Justification of cost per life

We assign the Future of Life Institute a **cost per microprobability of \$2.4 million**, with a {{PLAUSIBLE_RANGE}} of **\$1.2 million–\$12 million**. This is about twice the [AI Existential Risk](/cause/ai-risk) category default at the time of this writing, making FLI roughly half as effective per dollar. The estimate is very uncertain.

The higher cost reflects deployment uncertainty, not a claim that FLI's broad advocacy is intrinsically worse than technical alignment work. Public information does not let us verify that most of FLI's large 2021 windfall has been spent on actual AI-risk work. FLI's filings report large 2022 transfers to affiliated organizations, while FLI's public annual reports show much lower direct operating and program activity. Money that sits unspent, stays in affiliated entities, or pays out slowly has less expected value in a fast-moving AI-risk window.
