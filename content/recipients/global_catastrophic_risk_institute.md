_The following portfolio allocation was revised on August 4th 2026 by GPT-5, with prompts from Impact List staff._

- The allocation reflects GCRI's work around the 2020-2021 donations represented on the site, rather than its full history or the universe of topics on its website.
- AI risk receives 50% because GCRI's [2021 annual report](https://gcri.org/about/annual-report/2021) says its research concentrated heavily on AI, and all nine publications listed for that year concern AI. We do not use a higher share because the donations represented here also cover GCRI's more mixed 2020 portfolio, and the report attributes the 2021 concentration partly to restricted AI funding. For an unrestricted marginal donation, restricted funding can make non-AI work the more funding-sensitive margin; publication counts are not expenditure shares.
- Global Priorities receives 25% for cross-risk analysis, risk-and-decision research, and work that compares or improves understanding across global catastrophic risks.
- Meta-theory receives 10% for advising, field-building, and methodological work that does not map cleanly to one risk.
- Global Catastrophe Resilience receives 10% for GCRI's dedicated [resilience and recovery work](https://gcri.org/topics/resilience-recovery), including critical infrastructure, continuity, redundancy, refuges, food resilience, and recovery. This was a genuine program area but a minority of visible output during the relevant period.
- Nuclear receives 5% for nuclear-war and winter work visible in GCRI's [2020 annual report](https://gcri.org/about/annual-report/2020) and broader portfolio.
