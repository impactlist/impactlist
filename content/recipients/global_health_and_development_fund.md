# Internal Notes

The fund makes evidence-based grants aimed at improving health and economic empowerment in the world's poorest regions. The 80/20 split is an approximate mission mix: its work is predominantly health-focused, including malaria prevention and micronutrient fortification, with a smaller economic-development and policy portfolio. Sources: https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/funds/global-development, https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/payouts/march-2019-malaria-consortium, https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/payouts/august-2019-fortify-health, and https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/payouts/april-2019-instiglio
