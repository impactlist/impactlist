# Notes

Good Forever is modeled as 70% AI risk, 20% global-catastrophe resilience, 5% pandemics, and 5% science and technology. Its 2024 Form 990-PF reports about $1.59 million of direct AI-safety research, $255,000 of work on civilization robustness and governance, and $134,000 of biosecurity work. Its roughly $992,000 grant portfolio was also concentrated in AI safety and resilient hardware, with a smaller basic-science component. The rounded split combines those direct activities and grants; it does not count the grants again as donations from Blake Borgeson. ([2024 Form 990-PF](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/873744128/202523219349106922/full), [Good Forever programs](https://www.goodforever.org/foundation))
